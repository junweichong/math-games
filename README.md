# Math Games with Bad Coding

This repository contains a collection of digital adaptations of the brilliant games from Ben Orlin's book, **"Math Games with Bad Drawings"**. 

These games are designed to be simple to learn but deep in strategy, exploring concepts of spatial reasoning, logic, and arithmetic.

---

## 🕹️ Available Games

### 🟦 Spatial Games

#### 1. Dots and Boxes
A classic game of strategy and containment.
- **Goal**: Complete the most 1x1 boxes.
- **How to Play**: Players take turns connecting two adjacent dots with a line. If you complete a square, you score a point and must take another turn immediately.

#### 2. Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe
Tic-Tac-Toe, but with a meta-strategic layer.
- **Goal**: Get three big squares in a row on the 3x3 main board.
- **How to Play**: The board is a 3x3 grid of smaller Tic-Tac-Toe boards. Your move on a small board determines which small board your opponent must play in next. Winning a small board claims that square on the master board.

#### 3. Dandelions
A game of breezy expansion and garden dominance.
- **Goal**: Fill the board (Dandelion) or prevent it (Wind).
- **How to Play**: One player (Dandelion) plants a seed, then the other player (Wind) chooses a direction. ALL existing dandelions scatter seeds across the board in that direction.

### 🔢 Number Games

#### 4. Sequencium
A game of numerical sequence and territorial trapping.
- **Goal**: Reach higher numbers and build the longest sequence.
- **How to Play**: Players take turns claiming numbers. Each new number must be adjacent to one you already own and must follow the numerical sequence (increasing by 1).

#### 5. Prophecies
A game of numerical predictions and grid logic.
- **Goal**: Predict how many numbers will appear in each row and column.
- **How to Play**: Place numbers (1–5) or "X"s in a grid. No number can appear twice in the same row or column. Points are awarded for correct prophecies after the board is full.

#### 6. Pennywise
An economic game of spending big to take back small.
- **Goal**: Be the last player with coins remaining in your hand.
- **How to Play**: On your turn, place a coin from your hand into the pot. You may then take back any combination of coins from the pot as long as their total sum is **strictly less** than the value of the coin you just played.
